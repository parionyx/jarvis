## KEY LEARNINGS FOR THIS PROFILE CLASS

1. **60.2% + English-only + fully-funded = Italy ARDSU is the ONLY realistic path.** Every other major government scholarship has hard cutoffs (age, %, quota) that exclude this profile.

2. **IELTS is unavoidable for English-medium bachelor's** in Italy, China, Hungary. No MOI certificate from Hindi-medium school.

3. **UGC nomination is the real bottleneck for Hungary** — not university admission. 50 seats, pure merit, 60.2% = statistical near-zero.

4. **Russia government scholarship** (300 seats, no entrance exam, document screening) is fully funded but **>95% programs Russian-medium**. English bachelor's = mostly medical (MBBS), not CS.

5. **Spouse cannot accompany on student permit** in Italy/Hungary/Singapore/UK(bachelor). Both partners must apply as independent students.

6. **Budget reality:** ₹75-80k upfront for Italy (IELTS + flight + docs) is the minimum. No scholarship covers pre-departure costs.
