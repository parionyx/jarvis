## 🇭🇺 STIPENDIUM HUNGARICUM (Hungary)

**Official sources:** stipendiumhungaricum.hu (Tempus), ugc.gov.in (India Sending Partner), apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu

### Core facts (verified 21 Aug 2026)
| Item | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| What's covered | ✅ Free tuition (state uni) + ✅ HUF 43,700/mo living stipend + ✅ Accommodation (free dorm OR HUF 40,000/mo rent contribution) + ✅ Health insurance + ✅ 1-off travel allowance |
| India quota (UG) | ✅ **200 Bachelor's** seats (UGC notice) |
| **Application process** | ⚠️ **Dual — BOTH required**: (1) Tempus online system + (2) UGC paper package submission |
| Age limit | ❌ **None for bachelor's** (only under-21 for Turkey) |
| Academics (India) | ⚠️ **Min 60%** in last exam (UGC public notice: "at least 60% in the last examination passed") |
| **Language** | ⚠️ B2 English required — BUT: IELTS/TOEFL not mandatory at apply. Acceptable: recent English-medium school cert OR language test. Online interview covers English proficiency. |
| Language fallback | "I do not have a certificate yet" = upload declaration at apply; upload cert by 1 August (host-dependent) |
| Application window | Nov 2026 – **15 Jan 2027** (next) |
| NOMINATION | By end-Feb 2027 (UGC nominates from Indian applicants) |
| Entrance | March–May 2027 (host uni runs own Maths + interview; email contact) |
| Pass mark | Host exam min 56/100 points |
| Final result | Late June – mid-July 2027 |
| Arrival | Sept 2027 |

### Hungary — Why it fits YOU
- **60.2% = passes UGC 60% bar** ✅
- **No age cap** ✅
- **No passport required at apply** — Aadhaar/ID card + later-declaration accepted (Tempus guide) ✅
- English proof via interview if no cert ✅

### Process detail (must-do two routes): one form is NOT a submission.
> India partner is **UGC (University Grants Commission)** — not the Embassy. Tempus technical-eligibility ≠ UGC nomination. Both must complete.

### Pitfall (user-corrected)
> “full free rent covered” = FALSE. Official warning (stipendiumhungaricum.hu): contribution is **HUF 40,000/month OR free dorm** — does NOT cover full market rent in Budapest. Budget accordingly if off-campus.
