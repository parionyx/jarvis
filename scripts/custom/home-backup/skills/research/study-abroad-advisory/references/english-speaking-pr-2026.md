# English-Speaking PR-Track Fact Bank (2026-08-19)

Condensed, source-tagged facts for low-income applicants wanting English + PR.
Re-verify all figures annually on the official site.

## Canada — PREFERRED anchor
- Spouse of full-time student → open work permit (both can work).
- Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) up to 3 yrs.
- PR via Canadian Experience Class (Express Entry).
- Higher-degree partner (master's) should be the visa anchor to bring the other as dependant.

## Switzerland — REJECT for this profile
- VERIFIED (sbfi.admin.ch, Swiss Gov Excellence Scholarships): "mainly intended for
  talented early career researchers (ECRs) from abroad who have already completed their
  Master's degree" (max age 35). NO bachelor's / fresh-grad coverage. Application is free.
- Not English-speaking: official languages DE/FR/IT/RM.
- Settlement Permit C generally ~10 yrs residence + language integration.
- Cost of living among world's highest (~CHF 2,000+/mo).

## United Kingdom — weak for 'study together + PR'
- VERIFIED (gov.uk/student-visa/family-members): dependants allowed ONLY if you are a
  full-time postgraduate (RQF level 7+) course 9+ months, OR government-sponsored.
  A bachelor's (RQF 6) does NOT permit a dependant partner.

## Singapore — English ✓, PR uncertain
- VERIFIED (ica.gov.sg/reside/PR/apply): PR eligible if spouse of citizen/PR, unmarried
  child, aged parent of citizen, Employment Pass/S Pass holder, or student who passed a
  national exam (PSLE/GCE/Integrated Programme). Student pass generally does NOT sponsor spouse.
- PR is discretionary, not guaranteed.
- MOE Tuition Grant cuts undergrad fees but carries ~3-yr bond (prior knowledge).
