## REJECTED (Italian-only bachelor's)
Bologna CS, Pisa CS, Padova CS/CE, Torino CS, PoliTo CS, Milano Statale CS,
Bozen-Bolzano (only 4 non-EU seats + trilingual IT/DE/EN requirement).

## CRITICAL: Hindi-medium 12th → IELTS compulsory for the STUDENT VISA
- Even where admission needs no English cert (Ca' Foscari open access; Pavia via SAT), the
  **Italian Type-D student visa requires B2 language proof** per consulate checklist.
- Hindi-medium 12th = NO Medium-of-Instruction English certificate → must take IELTS (or other B2 cert).
- **English-medium applicants (e.g. Aarzoo) avoid this:** MOI accepted at most Italian unis for both
  admission AND visa. This is the single biggest divergence between the two applicants.
- Pavia admission: SAT ≥979 satisfies academic entry; IELTS is for the visa only.

## DSU / scholarship body per city
- Pavia → EDiSU Pavia (max band for lowest ISEE Parificato)
- Venice → ESU Veneto
- Rome → LazioDiSCo
- Trento → Opera Universitaria
- Milan → EDiSU Lombardia

All are Type-B post-arrival regional grants → consulate still needs pre-arrival proof of funds
(Indian income docs → ISEE Parificato via Italian CAF).

## Full cost to departure (one student, ₹60k income)
Passport ₹1.5k + SAT(waiver) ₹1.23k + IELTS ₹19k + prep ₹5k + apostille ₹8k + income cert ₹3.5k
+ uni fees ₹9k + insurance ₹15k + visa ₹4.5k + flight ₹45k ≈ **₹1.08L**.
After arrival DSU covers tuition + housing + meals + cash.
