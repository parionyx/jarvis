# Foreign Master's via Type A Fully-Funded Scholarship — 2027/28

Condensed from the 2026-08-24 session planning Aarzoo's foreign master's (₹0 personal). Re-verify all deadlines/amounts annually — they shift every cycle.

## Why Type A is the ONLY ₹0 path
- Type A = government scholarship award letter that the consulate accepts as **100% proof of funds**. No blocked account, no education-loan sanction, no personal bank balance.
- This is the sole legal way to satisfy "no visa-proof-of-bank-balance" AND "no loan" simultaneously. Every other route needs either cash-in-bank or a loan letter.
- IELTS is NOT required for either destination below — MOI certificate (or declaration + oral interview) substitutes.

## Master's document checklist (differs from bachelor's)
- B.Tech **degree certificate** (must clear backlog + have degree in hand BEFORE final admission)
- All-semester **transcripts**
- **MOI certificate** (English-medium proof from college)
- **Aadhaar** (ID fallback)
- **Passport** (apply early — longest lead time; Hungary allows apply-without, Türkiye needs for visa)
- **Motivation letter** (1 page, tailored per program)
- **CV / resume**
- **2 faculty recommendation letters** (standard for master's — verify per portal)
- (verify) study/research plan per program

## Pitfalls
- **Hungary "submitted" ≠ submitted**: Tempus form alone is NOT a submission. UGC India paper package is mandatory. Both or rejected.
- **Türkiye 75% is a hard master's floor** — bachelor's 70% does NOT carry over. Borderline 75.0x = thin margin; keep marksheet.
- **Degree must precede final admit** — a pending backlog (e.g. Nov exam) must clear and the certificate must exist before arrival-year conditions (1 Aug). Plan transcripts/degree by ~Apr 2027.
- **Spouse reunification BLOCKED during study** in both HU & TR (see SKILL.md Spouse rule). Canada is the exception (open work permit immediately).
