## 🛡️ 1. Meta Anti-Ban Compliance Rules
* **Pacing:** Wait 60 to 90 seconds between outgoing messages.
* **Batch Limit:** Max 15 to 20 cold messages per session.
* **Auto-Healing Bridge:** If bridge is disconnected, tool automatically self-heals in background.
* **Mandatory Opt-Out:** If prospect says "Stop", "No", or "Not interested", immediately reply with polite closure (*"Samajh gaya sir! Wish you all the best."*) and mark `⚫ Archived / Not Interested`. Never send a second message after an opt-out.
