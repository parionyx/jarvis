## 2. GOOGLE LOCAL SEARCH & GBP RANKING SCIENCE
Google Local Search rankings depend on 3 primary pillars:
1. **Relevance:** How well a Business Profile matches the search query. (Gaps: missing primary/secondary categories, missing detailed descriptions, missing website).
2. **Distance:** How close the business is to the searcher or specified locality. (Gaps: incomplete service area definitions).
3. **Prominence:** How well-known the business is online. (Gaps: low review count, unanswered reviews, lack of website authority, missing LocalBusiness Schema with NAP consistency).
* *Spam Policy:* Never add keyword stuffing to business names; never use doorway pages or fake reviews.
