# Skill: Lead Scoring & BANT Qualification Engine

This skill governs **Stage 4 (Score)** and **Stage 7 (Qualify)** of the Parionyx Sales Engine.

---

## 📊 1. Enterprise Scoring Model (1 to 10 Points)

Every lead extracted from Google Maps is evaluated on readiness, gap severity, and commercial value:

| Factor | Condition | Points Added |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Customer Demand** | Total Reviews > 10 & Rating >= 4.0 | **+4 Points** |
| **Clear Core Digital Gap** | Website Link is Missing on Google Maps Profile | **+3 Points** |
| **High-Ticket Local Niche** | Dental/Medical Clinic, Interior Studio, Real Estate, Auto Studio | **+2 Points** |
| **Direct WhatsApp Contact** | Verified 10-digit mobile number | **+1 Point** |

### 🚦 Score Gates:
* **Score 8–10:** 🟢 **Tier 1 Priority Lead** ➔ Immediate outreach in first batch.
* **Score 6–7:** 🟡 **Tier 2 Qualified Lead** ➔ Regular outreach queue.
* **Score < 6:** 🔴 **Unqualified / Low Value** ➔ Discard / Do not waste WhatsApp capacity.
